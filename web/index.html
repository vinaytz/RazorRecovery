Control is not a competitor. It is a randomly held-out slice of the same traffic that the engine is forbidden to touch, so the gap between it and Engine is the only number here that nothing else explains. Baseline is what a fixed retry-and-remind ladder gets. Oracle is what perfect play would have got, and it is the denominator — not a target.

Settled money, and how sure we are it belongs here

Every settlement is matched to a debt on a five-rung ladder. Levels 1–2 are exact ids. Level 3 matched a phone or email and an amount. Level 4 matched only an amount — that is a guess, and it is labelled one. Level 5 is a human's word for money we never saw arrive.

Abandoned checkouts

The second source of at-risk revenue, and the only one with no webhook. Nobody reports closing a tab, so this is an absence: an order was created and the payment never arrived. Watching is not at risk yet — most of it gets paid within the minute. Only the sweeper, 30 minutes later, turns silence into a case.

Stop recovery

Freezes deciding and executing, and cancels everything already queued. Webhooks, settlement matching and the abandonment sweep keep running — a paused engine that stops listening comes back to a ledger that drifted while it was off.
Or stop something narrower. A scoped pause leaves the rest of the ladder moving.
